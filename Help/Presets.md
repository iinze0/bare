# Presets

`p`, `g`, and `v` clear every tick, then turn a set on. You still press `a`.

## p performance

1 apps, 2 ads, 3 tasks, 5 telemetry, 6 search, 7 xbox, 8 gamedvr, 9 cpu, 11 hags, 12 visuals.

Search and Xbox services end disabled. Edge stays.

## g gaming

1 apps, 2 ads, 3 tasks, 8 gamedvr, 9 cpu, 11 hags, 12 visuals.

Xbox, Bluetooth, Search, Edge, Update, and Defender are not ticked. Audio services are not in the script, so they stay as Windows left them.

## v privacy

1 apps, 2 ads, 3 tasks, 4 privacy, 5 telemetry, 6 search.

No CPU plan change. No Edge removal unless you also tick 18.
