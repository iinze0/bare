# Recommendations

Gaming PC: press `g`, then `a`. That ticks apps, ads, tasks, Game DVR, CPU 100%, HAGS, and visual effects. Leave Xbox, Bluetooth, Search, Edge, Update, and Defender off the list.

Desktop you do not game on: press `p`, then `a`. That also disables Search, telemetry, and Xbox.

Privacy without breaking patches: press `v`, then `a`. Leave 19 and 20 off.

Do not tick 10 on a laptop. Do not tick 19 or 20 unless you already have another update and antivirus plan.
